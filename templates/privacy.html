Privacy

Short version: your bank PDFs are parsed in memory and not written to disk.

  • Uploaded PDFs stay in RAM for the request, then are discarded.
  • We do not create accounts or keep a transaction database in this MVP.
  • CSV/Excel downloads are rebuilt from data already in your browser session.
  • Payments are paused; when enabled later, card data will go through Paddle — never our servers.
  • We keep only aggregate counters (page views, parse success/fail, downloads) — no file contents, no filenames with account numbers.
  • Server access logs may record IP and User-Agent at the host layer — not file contents.

FAQ: Do you store my PDF? No. Parsed in memory, then discarded.

This is a product privacy notice for the MVP, not legal advice.
